📍 V24.1a Final Field-Test Navigation

V24 follows the actual GPX track loaded for the selected day and adds an offline vector map, live GPS marker, accuracy circle, route completion overlay, route labels, and map-follow controls, stores that track locally, matches the phone's GPS position to it, estimates remaining distance, detects sustained uphill sections and warns when you drift more than 35–50 m from the track.

Internet is not required for GPS route tracking after the route package is stored on the phone. Online map tiles, weather, cloud sharing and live group sync remain optional. OpenStreetMap-derived data must retain its required attribution and ODbL notice.
